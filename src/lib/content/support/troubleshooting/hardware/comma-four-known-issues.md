## Device won't turn on or keeps restarting

Check that the device cable and car harness connections are fully seated. Use the [setup guide](/setup) to check the installation. If the issue continues, [flash your comma](/support/troubleshooting/hardware/flash-your-comma) and install the latest stock openpilot release.

## Blank screen or camera issue

Reseat the device cable and check the mount using the [setup guide](/setup). If you use third-party software, restore the device and install the latest stock openpilot release before contacting support.

## Dark border at the bottom of the video display

This is a normal display effect that improves contrast behind on-screen elements such as the [torque bar](/support/using-openpilot/glossary).

## GPS isn't working

Make sure nothing blocks the device's view of the sky. If GPS still has trouble, include a photo of the device mounted on the windshield when contacting support.

## Car isn't recognized or openpilot won't engage

[Check that your car is supported](/vehicles), then check and reseat the harness and cable connections using the [setup guide](/setup). If the issue continues on the latest stock openpilot release, [capture a route](/support/troubleshooting/hardware/how-to-capture-a-route) showing the problem.

## Still having issues?

Try the steps in [hardware troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting/hardware#still-having-issues) before opening a support ticket. Include the steps you tried, relevant photos or videos, and a route if the issue happens while driving.
