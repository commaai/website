### known issues

::: dropdown openpilot won't update
Connect the device to Wi-Fi and read [updates and features](/support/using-openpilot/updates-and-features). If the software still cannot be updated, restore the device and install the latest stock openpilot release.

[learn how to flash your comma](/support/troubleshooting/hardware/flash-your-comma "card")
:::

::: dropdown installation or recovery is stuck
Restore your device to its factory state, then choose the latest stock openpilot release during setup.

[learn how to flash your comma](/support/troubleshooting/hardware/flash-your-comma "card")
:::

::: dropdown internet or mobile connection issues
Check the device's Wi-Fi connection first. Make sure your device is in an area where it has a strong signal. For prime cellular service and plan details, see [comma prime](/support/prime-connect/what-is-comma-prime). For comma connect features and device access, see [connect](/support/prime-connect/what-is-comma-connect).
:::

### have another issue?

We currently do not offer ticketed support for openpilot related issues. For issues concerning openpilot we suggest going to the [community discord](discord.comma.ai) and finding resources there or asking for help in the correct channels. Please respect community guidelines if doing so.
