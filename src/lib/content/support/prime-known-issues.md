::: dropdown comma prime SIM not connecting to network
If your device's comma prime subscription was enabled recently, leave your device connected to a strong Wi-Fi signal and wait at least a day for the SIM card to activate. Try rebooting your device after enough time has passed. Find an area with a strong mobile signal to make sure the issue is not due to weak cell coverage.

If your device is not on stock openpilot, try flashing it back to stock openpilot. [Learn how to flash your device](/support/flash-your-comma).
:::

::: dropdown comma prime SIM not working anymore
If your device is not on stock openpilot, try flashing it back to stock openpilot. [Learn how to flash your device](/support/flash-your-comma).

comma prime also maintains data limits to avoid abuse of comma prime (full) cell service. We reserve the right to block your cell usage if you are not on stock software (openpilot release version) and are deemed to be abusing our cell service.
:::

If the problem continues, follow the [troubleshooting and support steps](/support/products/comma-prime#troubleshooting).
