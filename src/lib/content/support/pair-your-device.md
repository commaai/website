## What is comma connect?

comma connect is the companion app for your device. It is completely optional to use, but gives you additional functionality such as viewing recorded drives and various remote features. If you want to learn more about what it is read our [connect article](/support/what-is-comma-connect) about it.

## How to pair to comma connect

First, you must complete device installation and follow the on-screen prompts on your comma device to connect to Wi-Fi and install openpilot. The [setup guide](/setup) walks through the process.

### on a comma four

Press the **settings** icon on the home screen, then navigate by swiping to the left to get to **device** settings. Once you are there press on **pair to connect** and scan the QR code that appears using your phones camera. This will take you to [connect.comma.ai](https://connect.comma.ai).

If you don't have an account you will be prompted to make one by logging in with you Google, Github or Apple account. Once you have created an account or logged in, your account will be paired with the device.

By default you can view the last 3 days of your recorded drives. Upgrade to [comma prime](/support/what-is-comma-prime) to get a full year of drives saved to the cloud.

Make sure your comma device has access to a strong wifi signal in order to upload your drives. You can also buy [comma prime](/support/what-is-comma-prime) to get 24/7 LTE on your device so your device can upload drives. Additionally, make sure that your [comma power](/support/products/comma-power) is connected to your car so that the device can upload drives when you are offroad and parked.
