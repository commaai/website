### what is openpilot?

openpilot is the officially supported software for the comma four. It uses the comma four's cameras and powerful computational power to upgrade your car's existing adaptive cruis control and lane centering systems to a hands-off, eyes-on L2 ADAS. The available controls and operating speeds of this system depends on your car and should be checked prior to your first drive by visiting the [supported cars page](/vehicles).

Although the system is designed for hands-free operation, you must keep your hands ready to steer and be prepared to brake or take over at any time. You can disengage openpilot using your car's controls or by pressing the brake at any time.

### how to engage openpilot

Once you have properly installed the device, turn on your vehicle and start driving as normal. You should see the device showing video of what it sees from the road on its screen. While driving, openpilot defaults to a disengaged state. It is only once you decide that you are ready to engage the device that it takes control of your car. To engage openpilot you must turn on your car's adaptive cruise control (ACC) system via the controls that usually reside on or around your steering wheel.
