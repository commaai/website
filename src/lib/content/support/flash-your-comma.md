---
title: Flash your comma
description: Restore your comma device to a clean factory state.
---

Flashing erases the software and data on your comma device and restores it to a clean factory state.

1. Open [flash.comma.ai](https://flash.comma.ai/) and follow the instructions shown there to flash your device.
    * **Do not** use the cable that came with your comma device; it is not a standard USB-C cable.
    * Use a high-quality USB-C cable, such as one that came with your phone or laptop.
    * Plug in the cables in the exact order shown in the flash.comma.ai instructions.
2. When the flash finishes, complete setup on the device.
3. Install the latest official openpilot release during setup.

If the issue continues on stock openpilot, return to [hardware troubleshooting](/support/comma-four-common-issues) and follow the steps to contact support.
