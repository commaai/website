---
title: How to pair your device
description: Pair your comma device with comma connect to view drives, manage prime, and use remote features.
order: 4
---

After installation, follow the on-screen prompts on your comma device to connect to Wi-Fi and install openpilot. The [setup guide](/setup) walks through the process and includes the **Pair your device with comma connect** step.

Open [comma connect](https://connect.comma.ai/) on your phone and follow the pairing prompt shown during device setup. Once paired, your device appears in connect so you can view recorded drives, manage prime, and access remote features.

If you need your dongle ID, open **Settings → Device** on the comma device.

If you need to reinstall device software, use the [software recovery page](https://flash.comma.ai/).
