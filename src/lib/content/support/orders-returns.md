## Orders

::: dropdown Do you offer bulk pricing or purchase orders?
A 10% discount is automatically applied to purchases of 10 or more eligible products; parts are excluded. If the discount is not applied, [contact support](https://commasupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new). For larger quantities, contact support to discuss deeper discounts that scale with quantity.

Orders must be placed through the comma shop, the official and only retailer of comma devices. Orders over $10,000 may be paid by purchase order and wire transfer; contact support with your item list or purchase order to get started.
:::

::: dropdown Do you offer discounts for military, seniors, or other groups?
comma does not offer discounts for specific groups.
:::

::: dropdown Do you offer upgrades or trade-in discounts for the comma two, three, or 3X?
Yes. See the [trade-in program](/shop/comma-four-trade-in) for details.
:::

::: dropdown What should I know before placing an international order?
Check that your car is supported at [comma.ai/vehicles](/vehicles). All support is for U.S. cars unless otherwise specified.

Prices do not include customs duties and taxes, which are set by your local customs authority and paid by you. These often range from $150–350 for a comma four and harness, but can be more depending on your country. The comma four HS code is **8525.81**. If you refuse to pay these fees, comma must pay them to receive the device back, and they will be deducted from your refund.

comma does not refund duties or taxes, since they are paid to your government. You may be able to claim a refund from your local customs office, subject to its policies. A link to your invoice is in your order confirmation email, and support can provide additional paperwork if needed.

Check your address carefully before ordering. It cannot be changed after the order is placed, and comma is not responsible for devices delivered to incorrect addresses.
:::

::: dropdown How can I cancel my order?
Open your order confirmation email, select **View Your Order**, and use the cancel option on the order page. You can cancel for a full refund until the order ships.

Orders cannot be modified. Cancel the order and place a new one with the correct items or information. Because some orders ship immediately, including on weekends or holidays, cancellation is not always possible.
:::

::: dropdown My order was marked as fraud. What should I do?
Our payment processor occasionally flags orders for suspicious activity. If this happens, [contact support](https://commasupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new).
:::

## Shipping

::: dropdown When will I receive my order?
Orders placed after 7:00 AM PT ship the next business day. You will receive an email with tracking information once your order ships; allow 1–2 days for tracking to become active.

Most orders arrive within 5–10 days unless expedited. comma is not responsible for delays in UPS 2nd Day Air delivery; choose it at your own risk. International delivery times vary. If your order has not shipped within a few business days, check your email for an update about inventory, an address correction, or payment verification. Shipping information is subject to change.
:::

::: dropdown I entered the wrong shipping address. What should I do?
For a U.S. order, [contact support](https://commasupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new) as soon as possible. We will try to recall the package and issue a refund after it returns to our warehouse, which can take 7–10 days or more. Place a new order after we confirm the recall.

International shipments cannot be recalled. Contact support and we will help where possible, but the customer is responsible for providing a correct delivery address.
:::

::: dropdown Where does my order ship from?
All orders ship from our warehouse in San Diego, California.
:::

::: dropdown Where do we ship?
We ship throughout the United States and to most countries. International shipping is a flat $30. We do not ship to PO boxes, except military addresses. U.S. deliveries currently require a signature, though this is subject to change. If you reject an international package, any additional fees will be deducted from your refund.

International customers are responsible for customs duties and taxes. These charges are not included in the purchase price and are not refundable by comma.
:::

::: dropdown What do I do if my device arrives damaged?
Report the damage within 3 days of delivery using the [shop support form](https://commasupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=29233377161623). Include photos of the device and its packaging. comma may not be responsible if you do not report damage within this window.

If a returned device arrives damaged, the repair cost will be deducted from your refund. comma is not responsible for items lost or damaged on their way back, so use the original packaging or pad the device carefully.
:::

::: dropdown My order has no recent tracking activity and is past the delivery date. What should I do?
Call UPS at [1-800-742-5877](tel:1-800-742-5877) or use its online contact form. Provide your tracking number and describe the issue. If UPS confirms the package is lost or delayed by more than one week, [contact comma support](https://commasupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new) so we can investigate and file a claim. We will provide updates and arrange any necessary reimbursement or replacement.
:::

::: dropdown My order tracking shows delivered, but the package hasn't arrived. What should I do?
If your order was sent by USPS, comma will respond to your support ticket after 3–4 days to see whether it has arrived; most delayed USPS packages do eventually arrive. For UPS packages, check whether tracking shows a pickup location. If a UPS delivery was signed for, it is considered delivered and there is little comma can do. If tracking shows the package returning to comma's warehouse, we will refund the order and recommend placing a new one.
:::

## Returns

If you are within 30 days of delivery, request a return at [comma.ai/returns](https://comma.ai/returns). Get the device in the mail within 30 days of receiving it.

- **U.S. returns:** A prepaid return label is available when you submit your request. If you use it, $9.99 will be deducted from your refund.
- **International returns:** You are responsible for shipping costs. Use tracking and insurance because comma is not responsible for lost or stolen packages. You will receive the return address after submitting your request.

Returns are usually processed within 2–5 business days after they arrive, and you will receive a confirmation email. Original packaging is not required, but pack the device carefully. If reusing a box, remove or cover old barcodes and labels.

[Start a return](https://comma.ai/returns "card")

### Return policy

UPS 2nd Day Air and international shipping fees are non-refundable. We reserve the right to cancel orders from customers who have made returns within 6 months of their most recent purchase. If you return only some of multiple eligible devices purchased at different prices, the refund uses the lowest eligible purchase price; returning all eligible orders receives a full refund.

Parts, repairs, and items marked **Final Sale** are ineligible for return. Only items purchased directly from the comma shop can be returned. comma is not responsible for damage caused by poor packaging or for items lost without tracking.

### International returns

You are responsible for shipping costs, duties, and customs fees when sending a device to comma for either a 30-day return or a warranty return. comma pays to send a repaired or replacement device back, which will be valued at $1. You remain responsible for all applicable taxes and duties.

Use tracking and insurance because comma is not responsible for lost or stolen packages. Mark the package **Return of Goods** and include HTS code **9801.00.10** on the customs form to avoid import charges. You are responsible for any duties and customs fees on the return.

Return instructions are provided after you [request a return](https://comma.ai/returns).

### Return FAQ
::: dropdown How long does it take to receive my refund?
Returns are usually processed within 2–5 business days after they arrive. The refund goes to your original payment method, and your bank may take up to another week to post it. If you have not received your refund within one week of the return being delivered, [contact comma support](https://commasupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new).
:::

::: dropdown Can I exchange my order?
comma does not offer exchanges. If there is an issue with your order, [request a return](https://comma.ai/returns) and place a new order.
:::

::: dropdown I'm adding support for a new car and would like to extend my return window.
If you're adding support for a new car, we can extend your return window to 90 days. Open a support [ticket](https://commasupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=29233363745559) with your order number once you've placed the order. Return window extensions are subject to approval.
:::

## Warranty and commacare

comma offers a one-year limited warranty against hardware defects. If you believe your device is defective, [open a hardware support ticket](https://commasupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=14150133300887). Read the article below for more information, including extended coverage with commacare.

[Basic warranty & commacare](/support/warranty "card")

### Is the 1-year hardware warranty transferable?
Yes. If your device was not purchased from the comma shop, the warranty still transfers, but you must provide the original order number and the email address associated with the device. comma cannot provide this information for you.
