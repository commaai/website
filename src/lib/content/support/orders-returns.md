## Orders and shipping

### Orders FAQ

::: dropdown When will I receive my order?
Orders placed after 7:00 AM PT ship the next business day. You will receive an email with tracking information once your order ships; allow 1–2 days for tracking to become active.

Most orders arrive within 5–10 days unless expedited. comma is not responsible for delays in UPS 2nd Day Air delivery. International delivery times vary. If your order has not shipped within a few business days, check your email for an update about inventory, an address correction, or payment verification. Shipping information is subject to change.
:::

::: dropdown How can I cancel my order?
Open your order confirmation email, select **View Your Order**, and use the cancel option on the order page. You can cancel for a full refund until the order ships.

Orders cannot be modified. Cancel the order and place a new one with the correct items or information. Because some orders ship immediately, including on weekends or holidays, cancellation is not always possible.
:::

::: dropdown I entered the wrong shipping address. What should I do?
For a U.S. order, [contact support](https://commasupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new) as soon as possible. We will try to recall the package and issue a refund after it returns to our warehouse, which can take 7–10 or more days. Place a new order after we confirm the recall.

International shipments cannot be recalled. Contact support and we will help where possible, but the customer is responsible for providing a correct delivery address.
:::

::: dropdown Where does my order ship from?
All orders ship from our warehouse in San Diego, California.
:::

::: dropdown Where do we ship?
We ship throughout the United States and to most countries. International shipping is a flat $30. We do not ship to PO boxes, except military addresses. U.S. deliveries require a signature. If you reject an international package, any additional fees will be deducted from your refund.

International customers are responsible for customs duties and taxes. These charges are not included in the purchase price and are not refundable by comma.
:::

::: dropdown Do you offer bulk pricing or purchase orders?
A 10% discount is automatically applied to purchases of 10 or more eligible products; parts are excluded. For larger quantities, [contact support](https://commasupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new).

Orders must be placed through the comma shop. Orders over $10,000 may be paid by purchase order and wire transfer; contact support with your item list or purchase order to get started.
:::

::: dropdown What do I do if my device arrives damaged?
Report the issue within three days of delivery using the [shop support form](https://commasupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=29233377161623). Include photos of both the product and its packaging. comma may not be responsible if you do not report damage within this window.

If a returned device arrives damaged, the repair cost may be deducted from your refund. comma is not responsible for items lost or damaged on their way back, so pack the device carefully.
:::

::: dropdown My order has no recent tracking activity and is past the delivery date. What should I do?
Call UPS at [1-800-742-5877](tel:1-800-742-5877) or use its online contact form. Provide your tracking number and describe the issue. If UPS confirms the package is lost or delayed by more than one week, [contact comma support](https://commasupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new) so we can investigate and file a claim. We will provide updates and arrange any necessary reimbursement or replacement.
:::

::: dropdown My order tracking shows delivered, but the package has not shown up yet?
If your order was sent by USPS, comma will respond to your support ticket after 3–4 days to see whether it has arrived; most delayed USPS packages do eventually arrive. For UPS packages, check whether tracking shows a pickup location. If a UPS delivery was signed for, it is considered delivered and there is little comma can do. If tracking shows the package returning to comma's warehouse, we will refund the order and recommend placing a new one.
:::

## Returns

If you are within 30 days of delivery, request a return at [comma.ai/returns](https://comma.ai/returns).

- **U.S. returns:** A prepaid return label is available when you submit your request. If you use it, $9.99 will be deducted from your refund.
- **International returns:** You are responsible for shipping costs. Use tracking and insurance because comma is not responsible for lost or stolen packages. You will receive the return address after submitting your request.

Returns are usually processed within 2–5 business days after they arrive, and you will receive a confirmation email. Original packaging is not required, but pack the device carefully. If reusing a box, remove or cover old barcodes and labels.

UPS 2nd Day Air and international shipping fees are non-refundable.

[Start a return](https://comma.ai/returns "external")

### Return policy

UPS 2nd Day Air and international shipping fees are non-refundable. comma may cancel orders from customers who have made a return within the previous six months. If you return only some of multiple eligible devices purchased at different prices, the refund uses the lowest eligible purchase price; returning all eligible orders receives a full refund.

Parts, repairs, and items marked **Final Sale** are ineligible for return. Only items purchased directly from the comma shop can be returned. comma is not responsible for damage caused by poor packaging or for items lost without tracking.

### International returns

You are responsible for shipping costs, duties, and customs fees when sending a device to comma for either a 30-day return or a warranty return. comma pays to send a repaired or replacement device back, which will be valued at $1. You remain responsible for all applicable taxes and duties.

Use tracking and insurance because comma is not responsible for lost or stolen packages. Mark the package **Return of Goods** and include HTS code **9801.00.10** on the customs form to avoid import charges. You are responsible for any duties and customs fees on the return.

Return instructions are provided after you [request a return](https://comma.ai/returns).

### Return FAQ
::: dropdown How long does it take to receive my refund?
Returns are usually processed within 2–5 business days after they arrive. The refund goes to your original payment method, and your bank may take up to another week to post it. If you have not received your refund within one week of the return being delivered, [contact comma support](https://commasupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new).
:::

::: dropdown Can I exchange my order?
comma does not offer exchanges. If there is an issue with your order, [request a return](https://comma.ai/returns) and place a new order.
:::
