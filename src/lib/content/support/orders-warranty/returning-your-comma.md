### Start a return

You can request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery at [comma.ai/returns](https://comma.ai/returns). The device must be mailed back within that 30-day window.

Only items purchased directly from the comma shop are eligible. Parts, repairs, and items marked **Final Sale** cannot be returned. We do not offer exchanges; return the original item and place a new order instead.

### Shipping your return

**U.S. returns**

A prepaid return label is available when you submit your request. If you use it, $9.99 will be deducted from your refund.

**International returns**

You are responsible for return shipping, duties, and customs fees. Use a tracked and insured service—comma is not responsible for packages lost or damaged in transit. Mark the package **Return of Goods** and include HTS code **9801.00.10** on the customs form. Return instructions and the shipping address are provided after you submit your request.

### Pack your return

Original packaging is not required, but pack the device carefully. comma is not responsible for damage caused by inadequate packaging. If you reuse a box, remove or cover every old barcode and shipping label.

### Refunds

Returns are normally processed within 2–5 business days after they reach our warehouse. You will receive a confirmation email when processing is complete, and your bank may take additional time to post the refund to your original payment method.

UPS 2nd Day Air and international shipping charges are non-refundable. Customs duties and taxes paid to your government are also not refundable by comma.

If you have not received a refund within one week of the return being delivered, [contact comma support](https://commasupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new) with your order number and return tracking.

### Return policy notes

- If multiple devices were purchased at different prices and are still eligible for return, a partial return is refunded at the lowest eligible purchase price. Returning all eligible orders receives a full refund.
- comma may cancel orders from customers who have made a return within the previous six months.
- Damage found on a returned device may be deducted from the refund.
