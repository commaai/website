Before driving, review the features and notes for your car on the [supported cars page](/vehicles). Learn how to engage and disengage openpilot using your vehicle's controls.

Stay attentive and ready to take control at all times. Read [Driving with openpilot](/support/using-openpilot/driving-with-openpilot) for an introduction to the driving features.
