comma prime is an optional subscription for your comma device. A subscription is not required to use openpilot or any core driving feature.

### prime

prime costs $24 per month and includes cellular data service in the United States. It also includes one year of drive storage in comma connect and eligible commacare coverage.

### prime lite

prime lite costs $14 per month and includes one year of drive storage and eligible commacare coverage, but it does not include a SIM card or cellular data service.

You can use Wi-Fi or install your own active SIM card. Third-party cellular service may require carrier and APN configuration, and comma does not provide setup or troubleshooting support for it.

### commacare

Both plans include commacare when the subscription is started within 30 days of receiving a comma four and remains continuously active. See [Basic warranty & commacare](/support/warranty#commacare) for coverage details.

You can activate or cancel either plan at any time through [comma connect](https://connect.comma.ai/).
