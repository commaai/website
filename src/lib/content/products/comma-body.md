## About comma body

comma body is a wheeled robotics development kit for comma 3X. It also works with comma four if you 3D print the [comma four body mount](https://github.com/commaai/hardware/blob/master/body/comma_four_stand.3mf).

[View the comma body firmware on GitHub](https://github.com/commaai/body "external")
