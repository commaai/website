## About your car harness

A car harness connects comma four to your vehicle. The correct harness depends on your exact model, model year, and factory equipment.

[Check car compatibility](/support/before-you-buy/check-car-support "article")

## Install your car harness

Use the setup guide for the connection points and installation steps for your vehicle.

[Install your comma and harness](/setup "article")

[Read the installation overview](/support/get-started/installation "article")

## Using your car harness

Once installed, check that each connector is fully seated before your first drive.

[Prepare for your first drive](/support/get-started/first-drive "article")

## Car harness troubleshooting

If your car is not recognized, confirm your exact car is supported, then reseat the harness and cable connections.

[Hardware troubleshooting](/support/troubleshooting/hardware "article")
