## About comma power

comma power connects the car harness to your vehicle's OBD-II port. It is optional and can be added after your device is installed.

With a comma power:
- The start and end of every drive are recorded.
- Your comma four remains powered and online while the car is off.
- Your comma four downloads updates while the car is off.

## Install comma power

Connect one end to your car's OBD-II port and the other to the harness box. That's it!

## Troubleshooting

If your device is not receiving power, check that both connections are fully seated. If that that is still not working, open a support ticket.
