## Maintaining your comma 3X

### Broken screen

Replacement screens for the comma 3X are available in the [shop](/shop/comma-device-screen). See the product page for details and repair terms.

### Replacing your SIM card

[How to replace the SIM card in a comma 3/3X](/support/replace-comma-3x-sim-card "card")

### Performing a Factory Reset

Reset the comma 3X by repeatedly tapping on the screen as soon as the device boots up. After that, the factory reset screen will come up.

If this does not work, a more complete factory reset can be performed at [flash.comma.ai](https://flash.comma.ai).

[Learn how to flash your comma](/support/flash-your-comma "card")

### In-warranty repairs

If you are having issues with your comma 3X and it is still in-warranty, you can open a support ticket for help.

Before opening a ticket:

1. Use only comma mounts, harnesses, and cables, and reseat all connections.
2. [Flash your device](/support/flash-your-comma) and install the latest stock openpilot release.
3. Confirm the issue still occurs, then open a ticket with the troubleshooting steps you tried.

[Open a support ticket](https://commasupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new "card")

### Out of warranty repairs

We offer trade-ins for eligible devices. See [upgrading to a comma four](#upgrading-to-a-comma-four) below for details.

## Upgrading to a comma four

You can trade in your comma 3X for $250 credit toward a new comma four, regardless of its condition. The offer is for the original purchaser of the comma 3X when buying a new comma four. Select the trade-in option on the comma four product page and send your 3X back within 30 days of receiving the new device.

[Read the trade-in instructions and terms](/shop/comma-four-trade-in).
