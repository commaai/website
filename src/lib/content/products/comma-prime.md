## Frequently asked questions

::: dropdown How do I claim the free prime offer?
Sign up for prime in [comma connect](https://connect.comma.ai/). With a purchase of a comma four, you get a free month of comma prime, which you can cancel at any time. After that, you will be charged $24 a month (domestic only).

Live outside the United States? You can still sign up for prime lite and optionally pair that with your own active SIM card. comma prime data service is available in the United States and Puerto Rico only.
:::

::: dropdown How do I transfer a prime subscription to a new device?
We aren't able to transfer comma prime subscriptions between devices. To do so, cancel your existing prime subscription through [comma connect](https://connect.comma.ai/) and sign up with the new device.

Your stored drives from the original device will not be transferred to the new device.
:::
