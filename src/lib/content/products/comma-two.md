## About comma two

Released in January 2020, comma two ran openpilot on the NEOS operating system. It is deprecated for new openpilot releases: v0.8.13 is the last version compatible with the device. [Read the comma two hardware notes](https://github.com/commaai/hardware/tree/master/comma_two).

## Maintaining your comma two

### Restoring factory software

If you need to restore or recover your comma two, follow comma's NEOS instructions for macOS, Linux, or Windows. The guide covers connecting the device to a computer, entering fastboot mode, and flashing the factory software.

[Restore your comma two with NEOS](https://github.com/commaai/eon-neos#readme "external")

## Upgrading to a comma four

You can trade in your comma two for $250 credit toward a new comma four, regardless of its condition. The offer is for the original purchaser of the comma two when buying a new comma four. Select the trade-in option on the comma four product page and send your comma two back within 30 days of receiving the new device.

[Read the trade-in instructions and terms](/shop/comma-four-trade-in).
